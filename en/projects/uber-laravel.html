Uber Laravel

Laravel transport platform covering users, trips, payments, and GDPR-minded data handling.

View on GitHub

Overview

PHP/Laravel backend for a ride-hailing style domain: passengers, drivers, vehicles, reservations, and payments on PostgreSQL.

Includes native auth flows, Stripe-related payment paths, unit tests, and modeling artifacts (PDM, use cases) used during design.

Features

  • User roles · Registration and auth for passengers and drivers via Laravel auth.
  • Trips and bookings · Reservation and trip tracking with payment hooks.
  • Ratings · Reviews between drivers and passengers after rides.
  • Geo analysis · Trip geography used for reporting and route insight.
  • GDPR controls · Encryption and privacy-oriented data handling for personal fields.
